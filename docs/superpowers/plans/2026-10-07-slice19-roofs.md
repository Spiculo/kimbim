# Slice 19 — Roofs

**Goal:** a house gets a real roof: flat, or pitched (shed, gable, hip) from its outline, with the walls reaching up to it (Archicad's Roof tool and *Trim to Roof*).

## Decisions (D66)

| Topic | Archicad behaviour followed | KIMCAD |
|---|---|---|
| Tool | Roof tool: single-plane or multi-plane roof drawn by its outline; pitch per edge; gable ends | Roof tool **Shift+R**: draw the eaves outline like a slab; toolbar kind (Flat / Shed / Gable / Hip) and pitch; the eaves height comes from the height field (above the level). The bridge also takes one slope per edge (`null` = gable end) for any multi-plane roof |
| Presets | — | Shed: the longest edge slopes; Gable: the edges parallel to the longest slope (ridge along the long side); Hip: every edge |
| Shape | Multi-plane roof planes meet at ridges and hips | Lower envelope of the edge planes (each rises from its edge at its slope), computed by clipping the outline per plane; pitched roofs need a convex outline (an L-shaped house is two roofs, as Archicad users often do) |
| Body | Roof with thickness | A plate of constant vertical thickness as `IfcPolygonalFaceSet` (closed, no T-junctions); flat roofs are an extruded outline. Volume = outline area × thickness |
| IFC | Roofs export as `IfcRoof` (or IfcSlab ROOF parts) | `IfcRoof` with its own body (as Bonsai makes them), predefined type FLAT_ROOF / SHED_ROOF / GABLE_ROOF / HIP_ROOF / FREEFORM, in the level; definition in `KIMCAD_Roof` (outline relative to the roof, slopes, thickness) so a moved roof stays consistent |
| Walls | *Trim to Roof* (Connect menu) | *Model › Trim walls to roof* (select a roof + walls): walls are raised to reach the underside and clipped by its planes (`IfcBooleanClippingResult`; ifcopenshell's `BBIM_Boolean` keeps the clips through wall rebuilds). `KIMCAD_RoofAttach` keeps the link: after any edit moving the roof or the wall, the wall is re-trimmed in the same undo step; deleting the roof (or *Release walls from roof*) puts walls back at their own height |
| Drawings | Roofs show in sections and elevations | The SVG serializer draws them like any element (the sample house's south elevation and Section 1 show the gable roof) |

## Done

- [x] `kimcad/roofs.py`: `create_roof`, `roof_info`, `attach_walls_to_roof`, `detach_walls_from_roof`, after-action re-trim hook.
- [x] App: Roof tool (Shift+R) with kind and pitch in the toolbar, Model › Trim walls to roof / Release walls from roof; Spanish strings ("Cubierta", "a dos aguas", "a cuatro aguas").
- [x] Sample house: a 30° gable roof with 0.5 m eaves on the Roof level, Level 2 walls trimmed to it (M3 validation, web-ifc and the M4 sheet pass).
- [x] Sheets: when no free spot is left, a new drawing goes where it overlaps the others least (it used to land on top of the first drawing).
- [x] Rooms: the stamp sits where the room was clicked (Archicad's zone reference point).
- [x] Tests: Python `test_roofs.py` (7: flat; hip/gable/shed volume, height and ridge length; per-edge slopes and refusals; walls trimmed under the eaves and gable, following a moved roof and a moved wall, undo, release, roof deletion, IFC validation; roof in a section), app `roofsAndTrimToRoof`.

## Not done (later)
