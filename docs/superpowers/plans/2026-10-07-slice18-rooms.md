# Slice 18 — Rooms and areas

**Goal:** an architect clicks inside walls and gets a room with its name, number and area on the plan and in a schedule (Archicad's Zone tool, "magic wand" placement).

## Decisions (D65)

| Topic | Archicad behaviour followed | KIMCAD |
|---|---|---|
| Tool | Zone tool; click inside walls (Inner Edge / magic wand) | Room tool, shortcut **Z**; one click inside the walls |
| Shape | The zone polygon follows the inner faces of the enclosing walls | Wall faces (both sides of each axis, extended past the ends, plus end caps) form a planar graph; the room is the face of the graph around the clicked point. Clicks outside the building or inside a wall are refused, and so is a click inside an existing room |
| IFC | Zones export as `IfcSpace`: zone number → `Name`, zone name → `LongName` | Same; `PredefinedType` INTERNAL; aggregated to the level (`IfcRelAggregates`); body = the outline extruded to the next level (3 m on the top level) |
| Number | Automatic numbering | 101, 102… on the first level, 201… on the second; editable |
| Areas | Zone area, perimeter, height | `Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities`: NetFloorArea, GrossFloorArea, NetPerimeter, Height |
| Updating | *Update Zones* re-detects the boundary | Automatic: the clicked point is stored (`KIMCAD_Room.SeedX/SeedY`) and after any edit that changes walls on a level, its rooms re-fit **in the same undo step** (a bridge after-action hook). A room whose walls no longer enclose it keeps its last shape |
| Stamp | Zone stamp: name, number, area | Plan view: name / number / area at the room's centroid (dotted outline, picked by its stamp only). Floor plan drawings: the same stamp in the overlay |
| Schedule | Interactive Schedule › Zones, exportable | *File › Room schedule…*: number, name, level, area, perimeter, height, total; *Export CSV…* (UTF-8, total row) |
